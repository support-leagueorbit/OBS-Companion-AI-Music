# Changelog

## 0.3.1

Sound improvements. Nothing to change in your setup; sound packs from sounds-v1 work as before.

- Dynamics: choruses and climaxes come forward and intros and verses sit back, with a ramp into each chorus. Loop styles build and release over the tune, and Cinematic builds to a real climax.
- Longer tunes with a fuller arc: about 90 seconds for loop styles and 105 for song styles, so songs get a second chorus. The first tune after you press play or change style stays at the shorter length, so it starts as quickly as before.
- Groove: each style family now has a consistent feel (laid back for lo-fi, hip hop and R&B; pushed for rock and drum & bass; a pocket for pop, funk and folk; tight for electronic), in place of random timing.
- Mix per style: lo-fi is less muddy and less dull, and wide styles such as Cinematic are less washed out, with a firmer centre. Each correction is measured on the tune and applied only as far as needed.
- Rooms: dry recorded instruments (piano, harp, mallets, acoustic guitars) get a small room so they sit with the rest; electric guitars get a short amp-room sound; orchestral parts get less added reverb, because their hall is already in the recording.

## 0.3.0 (first public release)

- 21 styles: Lo-fi, Chillhop, Synthwave, Ambient, Deep House, Cinematic, Drum & Bass, Trap, Medieval/Fantasy, Rock, Soft Rock, Pop, Funk, R&B / Neo-soul, Folk / Acoustic, Blues, Jazz (swing, ballad, bossa nova, up-tempo), Electronic (French house, industrial, robotic), Hip Hop (West Coast, boom-bap, hard), Electro Hip Hop and Rap Rock.
- Song form for the song styles (intro, verses, choruses with a hook, bridge or solo, outro), with drum fills and crashes at section changes.
- Subtle variation: each tune of a style shifts a little in tempo and energy, and within a tune the drums vary and some instruments rest or come in late.
- Add, remove, mute and mix instruments while the music plays. Changes arrive in a few seconds.
- Save your own styles as one-click buttons; export and import them.
- **Try** and **Sound check** to hear any instrument or drum kit on its own.
- Optional recorded sounds in 12 packs (piano, keys, harp, mallets and percussion, drums, acoustic, electric and rock guitars, bass, orchestra, winds, synths). Install any combination; each is checked by SHA-256.
- Export the current tune as 24-bit WAV, stems (ZIP) or MIDI.
- Mastered to about -14 LUFS for streaming.
- OBS: Browser Source, Media Source, or built into OBS Companion with a style per scene.
- Windows: native window, plays to your speakers, tray icon, start at sign-in, installs and uninstalls per user without administrator rights.
- macOS and Linux: runs in the browser.
- One-line installers for Windows (PowerShell) and macOS/Linux (sh). They download only from GitHub Releases and check every checksum.
