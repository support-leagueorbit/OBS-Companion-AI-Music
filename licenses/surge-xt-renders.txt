Surge XT recordings (sound folders surge-*)
==========================================

These instruments are audio recorded note by note from Surge XT 1.3.4, the free and open
source synthesizer (https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/, source
https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/surge, GPL-3.0-or-later), playing its own factory
patches. Surge XT was built from its official 1.3.4 source release and played through its
Python module. No Surge XT code or patch file is distributed with AI Music; only the
recorded WAV files and AI Music's own SFZ key maps.

The GPL covers the program, not the sound it produces: audio made by playing a GPL
synthesizer is the player's own output (GNU GPL FAQ, "Is there some way that I can GPL
the output people get from use of my program?"). The recorded notes are therefore
distributed with AI Music under its own terms, with this credit.

Recording: 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo, one note every three semitones at one to three
velocities, with the patches' reverb and delay switched off (AI Music adds its own room).
Each instrument is normalised as a whole, so velocity differences are kept.
